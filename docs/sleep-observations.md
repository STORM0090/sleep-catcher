## What the notes show

Seven nights count: 1 to 9, minus night 7 (I never wrote down the outcome)
and night 8 (something outside sleep itself that is known to affect dreams).

- The last wake-up was gradual, with several attempts, and in the end I got
  up on my own: I remembered a dream on 5 of 5 nights (1, 2, 4, 6, 9).
- The last wake-up was one clean or sharp moment: no dream on 2 of 2 nights
  (3, 5).

Sleep score, REM share and total sleep did not line up with this at all. The
worst night (score 40) gave the most vivid dream, while two of the best nights
(81 and 88) gave nothing. What seemed to matter was how the night ended.

Seven nights is a tiny sample. This is a hypothesis for the app to test, not
a result.

## What the app does with it

The "Catch a dream" mode tries to reproduce the good nights: a soft vibration
on a cycle boundary to bring me closer to the surface, and a loud alarm only
as a safety net at the latest time I can get up. The "Refreshed" mode does the
opposite on purpose: one alarm on a cycle boundary and nothing else.
